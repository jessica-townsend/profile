I'm Jessica Townsend, a full-stack web developer with five years of experience across WordPress, Shopify, and modern JavaScript, recently focused on integrating AI agents in development and content workflows.

this is me

I graduated from Purdue University in May 2021 with a B.S. in Web Programming & Design and a B.S. in Virtual Product Integration.

Currently, I'm a full-stack developer at the Thought and Expression Company, where my recent work has included a Next.js dashboard that consolidates data from multiple sources for in-house analysis, custom Shopify app development against the GraphQL Admin API, and custom WordPress plugin and Gutenberg block development. I also have prior experience at SFP, an agency based in Lafayette, IN, building and maintaining sites across a range of industries.

On the AI side, I design and build workflows around Claude Code, including custom skills for things like pre-meeting work summaries and GitHub issue implementation. I've also built Claude integrations for Shopify and WordPress that let agents publish posts and push updates directly.

I live in southwest Washington with my dog. In my free time I enjoy traveling, indoor rock climbing, and getting out to hike and camp in the national parks I moved out here for.
